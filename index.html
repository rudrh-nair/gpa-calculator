How Weighting Works

Weighted GPA is your unweighted GPA plus a bump for each course:

  • Honors: +0.05
  • AP / UConn ECE / NCC / IBDP: +0.07
  • Honors Civics 1 and 2: +0.025 each

Some course names don't say what they count as:

  • Data Structures: Honors
  • Multivariable Calculus: AP
  • All Agriscience courses: Honors
